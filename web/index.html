                                                                                    Check everything

                                                                                    Tests every connection (Google, Home Assistant, the assistant, notifications, speakers, backups) and says what to fix.

                                                                                      Live test with our real accounts

                                                                                      Tries every Gmail, Tasks and Calendar feature on each connected account: adds a test to-do, a test calendar entry at 3am in a month's time and a test Gmail draft, checks each one, then deletes them. Nothing is ever sent.

                                                                                        Backups

                                                                                        Every night at 2:40am an encrypted copy of Life OS goes to a folder off this computer's own disk: a USB stick, or a folder that Google Drive, iCloud Drive or OneDrive keeps in sync. Each copy is opened again and checked.

                                                                                        For example /Volumes/USB STICK or ~/Library/CloudStorage/GoogleDrive-you@gmail.com/My Drive/Life OS

                                                                                        At least 10 characters, like three random words. Write it down somewhere safe, not on this computer: without it the backups can't be opened. Leave blank to keep the one you set.

                                                                                        Restore from a backup

                                                                                        Replaces everything in Life OS with the backup when it next starts. What's here now is kept in a "before-restore" folder, not deleted.

                                                                                        Updates and this computer

                                                                                        Got an update file, like lifeos-update-0.17.0.zip? Choose it here. Your settings and history are kept, and if it doesn't start properly Life OS goes back by itself.

                                                                                        Keys and addresses

                                                                                        Voice and speakers

                                                                                        Google speakers can't pass what you say straight to Life OS (Google stopped that for all apps in 2023), so they go through Home Assistant: fixed phrases answered out loud, and announcements. For saying anything at all, use Home Assistant's own voice assistant: "ask Life OS to move the dentist to Friday". Things Gemini adds to Google Tasks come into Life OS too, and shopping ones go on the Shopping list.

                                                                                        Connect Home Assistant first (Keys and addresses, above). Its Google Cast integration finds your Google speakers.

                                                                                        For example tts.home_assistant_cloud or tts.google_translate_en_com. Blank uses Google Translate's voice.

                                                                                        Phrases for your Google speakers

                                                                                        In the Google Home app: Automations, add, Household. Starter: "When I say to my assistant" and the phrase. Action: Adjust home devices, Scenes, then the Life OS scene. One routine per phrase you want.

                                                                                          Set up Home Assistant (copy and paste)

                                                                                          Then restart Home Assistant and expose the "Life OS ..." buttons to Google Home (Settings, Voice assistants, Expose).

                                                                                          Talk to Life OS on a Home Assistant Voice speaker

                                                                                          Life OS becomes a conversation agent in Home Assistant, so a Home Assistant Voice speaker (or a Nest Mini with a replacement board) talks to Life OS directly and keeps listening when Life OS asks a question back. Money, messages, unlocking and the alarm still wait for a tap on a phone.

                                                                                          1. Download the integration below and unzip it.
                                                                                          2. Copy the "lifeos" folder into Home Assistant's config/custom_components folder (the File editor or Samba add-on makes this easy), then restart Home Assistant.
                                                                                          3. In Home Assistant: Settings, Devices and services, Add integration, Life OS. Give it Life OS's address and the household key.
                                                                                          4. Settings, Voice assistants, your assistant: choose Life OS as the conversation agent.
